- **Create unlimited trees** — each with its own canvas, people, and relations

- **Sort** by creation date or alphabetically
- **Search** through your trees by name

### 👤 Character Creation

- **Portrait via clipboard** — just copy an image and paste it into the app. You can **zoom and drag** the image to frame it perfectly
- **Gender** — male or female
- **Name** — anything you want
- **Deceased marker** — check a box if the character is dead, and their portrait turns black & white
- **Occult type** — human, witch, fairy, vampire, or any custom type you create
- **Career** — pick from saved career templates
- **Education** — pick from saved education templates
- **Dream** — pick from saved dream templates
- **Traits** — add **multiple** character traits, picked from saved templates
- **Smart autocomplete** — once you save a template (e.g. "Kind"), start typing "Ki..." and it appears in a dropdown. Traits can have many; everything else is one per slot
- **Pets** — separate tab with name, traits, gender, and portrait. Pet icon appears at the **top-right** of the character's portrait; occult icon appears at the **bottom-left**
- **Oracle** — On the top right, there is an eye icon that can randomly fill in some functions for you. The right mouse button will give you a choice that the Oracle can randomize

### 🐾 Pets

- **Fully custom** — name, gender, character trait, and portrait
- **Visual badge** — pet's portrait (or gender symbol) appears at the **top-right** of the owner's card

### 🧙 Occults

- **Create custom occult types** — human, witch, fairy, vampire, werewolf, mermaid, whatever you imagine
- **Custom icons** — paste an image, crop it in a circle, and it becomes the occult badge
- **Sort & search** — find your occults by name or date

### 🔗 Relations

- **Custom relation types** — married, dating, best friends, rivals, anything you want
- **Default relation types** — parent and sibling are built in
- **Customizable lines** — change line color and style:
  - Solid
  - Dashed
  - Dotted
  - Dash-Dot
  - Zigzag
- **Multiple relations** between the same two characters — lines curve around each other
- **Relation labels** — text labels on each line that you can **drag along the line**
- **Drag the whole line** — move relation lines to reshape them; the lines follow the characters when you move them

### ➕ Quick Add Buttons on Characters

Every character card has four small **+** buttons around it:

- **+ Up** — add a parent
- **+ Down** — add a child
- **+ Left** — add a sibling
- **+ Right** — add a friend, partner, or anyone else you choose

### 🎨 Customization

- **Color theme** — pick a color on the wheel, and the entire palette (background, panels, borders, text) adapts automatically
- **Reset to default gold** — one click to return to the original Ametysta theme
- **Background image** — paste an image, and it becomes the canvas background; remove it any time
- **Card frame styles** — choose how character portraits are framed
- **Background patterns** — square grid, diagonal grid, waves, or none
- **Background animations** — subtle animated effects like falling leaves
- **Hide interface mode** — press the monitor button to hide all UI and just look at your tree. **Right-click** to bring the interface back

### 🔄 Quality of Life

- **Autosave** — everything is saved automatically; when you close the app and reopen it, your work is exactly where you left it
- **Reload button** — the arrow button at the top-right force-reloads the app if something lags or doesn't load
- **GitHub heart button** — quick link to the source repository on GitHub
- **Search & sort everywhere** — every panel (trees, relations, occults, templates) supports sorting by date or alphabetically, and has a search bar to filter what you need
- **Template saving** — save careers, education, dreams, traits, and pet traits once, and reuse them forever
- **Zoom canvas** — mouse wheel to zoom in and out
- **Pan canvas** — click and drag the empty space to move around
- **Free movement** — drag characters and lines anywhere; everything updates in real time
